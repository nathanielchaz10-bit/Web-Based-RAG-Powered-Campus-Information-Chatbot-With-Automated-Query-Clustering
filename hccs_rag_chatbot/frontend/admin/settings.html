Rate Limiting & Abuse Protection

Per-Student Limit 10
Max queries one student may send per time window (abuse / DDoS guard).
Time Window (seconds) 60
The rolling window the per-student limit is counted over.
Server-Wide Limit (/ min) 30
Max queries the whole server accepts per minute across all students, protecting Gemini capacity.
Per-Student Limit (/ day) 20
Questions one student may ask per day. Spreads a limited budget fairly. 0 = unlimited.
Server-Wide Limit (/ day) 300
The main budget guard: total questions/day across everyone. Sized so daily × days stays under your Gemini credit. 0 = unlimited.
Chatbot Availability Turn the student chatbot off instantly for everyone (e.g. when the budget is spent) without stopping the server.

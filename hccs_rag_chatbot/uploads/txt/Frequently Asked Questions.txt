HOLY CHILD CATHOLIC SCHOOL — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

This document contains official answers to common questions from students,
alumni, and parents. Answers should be given formally and completely.


SCHOOL OFFICE HOURS
Question: What are the school's office hours?
Answer: The school office is open from 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
The office is closed on weekends and on official school holidays.


CONTACTING THE SCHOOL
Question: How can I contact the school? What is the school's email address?
Answer: The school may be reached through its official email address,
hccs1945@hccs.edu.ph. For office-hour inquiries, please refer to the school
office hours above.


CONTACTING THE REGISTRAR'S OFFICE
Question: How do I contact the Registrar's Office? What is the Registrar's email?
Answer: The Registrar's Office may be reached through its official email
address, registrar@hccs.edu.ph. Please direct requests for records, transcripts,
and Form 137 to this office.


CONTACTING THE ALUMNI OFFICE
Question: How do I contact the Alumni Office? What is the Alumni Office email?
Answer: The Alumni Office may be reached through its official email address,
hccsalumni.relations@hccs.edu.ph.


REQUESTING A FORM 137 (PERMANENT RECORD)
Question: How do I request my Form 137? What are the requirements, fee, and
processing time?
Answer: To request a Form 137, please observe the following:

  1. Requirement (for alumni): Submit a request letter from your school of
     origin.
  2. Processing fee: PHP 150.00.
  3. Processing time: Please allow 3 working days before the document can be
     released.
  4. Claiming: A claim stub will be issued upon request. Kindly provide a
     contact number so the Registrar's Office can reach you for confirmation
     and updates.

Requests for Form 137 are handled by the Registrar's Office
(registrar@hccs.edu.ph).
